01 — About

Who Am I?

Dhaka, Bangladesh

I'm a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET) driven by the desire to design, simulate, and build intelligent physical systems that bridge mechanical precision and embedded autonomy.

My engineering work spans across multiphysics FEA/CFD simulation (thermal-structural interactions, viscoelasticity, convective heat transfer) and full-stack robotic prototyping (precision gear train fabrication, real-time celestial tracking kinematics, and embedded microcontrollers like ESP32/STM32).

Beyond mechanical design, I am an enthusiastic competitive programmer who has qualified for Round 1 of Google Code Jam and participated in Google Kick Start. I am also an officially rated FIDE chess player, appointed Chess.com Campus Ambassador for BUET, and an avid traveller who represented Bangladesh at the International Blockchain Olympiad in Amsterdam.

2024 – 2026 Robotics & Optics

"Beyond Horizon" — Automated Smart Alt-Azimuth Robotic Mount

Automated Celestial Acquisition & High-Precision Tracking System

Engineered and fabricated a dual-axis motorized robotic mount for a custom refractor telescope (D80F900 doublet optical lens assembly). Synthesized custom high-ratio reduction gear trains in SolidWorks to minimize backlash and dampen tracking vibrations.

Programmed embedded C++ firmware on an ESP32 executing real-time equatorial coordinates (Right Ascension / Declination) to topocentric horizon coordinates (Altitude / Azimuth) transformations. Validated structural deflection and thermal gradient effects in COMSOL Multiphysics to guarantee optical axis collinearity.

ESP32 Firmware SolidWorks CAD COMSOL FEA Coordinate Kinematics Precision Steppers Closed-Loop Control

FEA & Reliability Simulation

Thermomechanical Simulation of Power Module Solder Joints

Constructed a 2D half-symmetry coupled thermal-structural FEA model in COMSOL Multiphysics replicating failure mechanisms in Single-Sided Cooling (SSC) power modules. Implemented nonlinear Anand Viscoplasticity for 60Sn40Pb solder and Morrow fatigue model to predict thermal cycle lifetimes under cyclic thermal loading (ΔTj ≈ 120°C).

COMSOL Multiphysics Anand Viscoplasticity Morrow Fatigue Thermal-Stress Coupling
Computational Mechanics Engine

Beam Deflection Solver via Macaulay's Singularity Method

Developed a unified Python structural analysis engine utilizing Macaulay bracketed singularity functions. Eliminates manual multi-segment boundary matching and automates continuous shear force diagrams (SFD), bending moment diagrams (BMD), and elastic deflection profiles under complex multi-point loads and moments.

Python NumPy Matplotlib Boundary Value Formulations

Architected a cross-platform desktop application designed for mathematical derivation and live STEM annotation. Engineered an instant LaTeX typesetting engine utilizing KaTeX coupled with low-latency stylus and canvas rendering to handle intricate multi-line formulas seamlessly.
